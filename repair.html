FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

How long does a repair take?

Most repairs are completed within 2-3 weeks from the day we receive your shoes. Complex custom work may take longer, and we'll notify you of any delays.

What's covered under warranty?

Our lifetime warranty covers manufacturing defects and workmanship issues including sole separation, stitching failures, and material defects. Normal wear and tear is not covered but can be repaired for a fee.

How much do repairs cost?

Warranty repairs are free. Non-warranty repairs range from $30-$120 depending on the type of work needed. We'll provide a quote before starting any paid work.

Can I track my repair?

Yes! Once we receive your shoes, you'll get a tracking number to monitor your repair status online. We'll email you at each stage of the process.
